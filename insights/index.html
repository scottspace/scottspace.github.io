Three things I found

3.9x Energy and Transportation produce nearly four AI winners for every underperformer. Finance and Retail are inverted. Physical-asset sectors dominate.
3.4% Of 1,376 AI vendors tracked, only 47 serve both high-performing and underperforming companies. The vendor market has split into two parallel ecosystems with almost no overlap.
+0.02 Companies with no reported AI activity carry a higher mean Information Ratio than those deploying AI. Most companies are doing AI badly. The quiet ones are outperforming.
